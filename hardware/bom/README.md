# Bill of materials

The electrical, control and mechatronic purchase BOM is [bom.csv](bom.csv).
The consolidated mechanical manufacturing and fastener list is
[mechanical-bom.csv](mechanical-bom.csv). Items marked `Main BOM reference` in
the mechanical list must not be purchased a second time.

Estimated costs in `bom.csv` are gross product prices for the full quantity
shown in each row. They exclude delivery and were checked on 2026-10-30. Prices
and availability may change, so confirm them before ordering. Mechanical
manufacturing prices depend on the selected supplier and are not estimated.
